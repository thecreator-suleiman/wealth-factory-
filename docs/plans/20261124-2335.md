# Free business plan: How to Start a Business Under $200

# Start a Business Under $200: Your Simple Plan

This plan will help you launch a business with very little money.

## The Idea in One Line

Offer a useful service or product that people need and are willing to pay for, using skills you already have or can learn quickly.

## Who Pays and Why

*   **People who need a solution:** They have a problem you can solve.
*   **People who want convenience:** They don't have time or skills to do it themselves.
*   **People looking for value:** They want something good at a fair price.

**Why they pay:** Because you offer them something they want or need, and you make their lives easier or better.

## Start-up Cost (Low/Realistic, in USD)

**Goal: Under $200**

*   **Materials/Supplies:** $50 - $100 (e.g., cleaning supplies, craft materials, ingredients, basic tools)
*   **Marketing (very basic):** $0 - $20 (e.g., printing flyers, small social media ad budget)
*   **Transportation (if needed):** $0 - $30 (e.g., bus fare, bike maintenance)
*   **Contingency (unexpected costs):** $20 - $50

**Total: $70 - $200**

## Step-by-Step: First 30 Days

1.  **Week 1: Define Your Service/Product:**
    *   What can you do well? (e.g., cleaning, tutoring, making crafts, fixing things, delivering food, social media help)
    *   What do people in your community need?
    *   Choose ONE thing to focus on.

2.  **Week 2: Get Your Tools Ready:**
    *   List everything you need.
    *   Buy the cheapest, most essential items first.
    *   Borrow or use what you already have.

3.  **Week 3: Tell People What You Do:**
    *   Make simple flyers or posters.
    *   Tell friends, family, and neighbors.
    *   Create a free social media page.

4.  **Week 4: Do Your First Jobs:**
    *   Offer a small discount for your first few customers.
    *   Do a great job!
    *   Ask for feedback.

## How to Get the First 10 Customers

1.  **Start with your network:** Ask friends, family, and neighbors.
2.  **Offer a "first-time" discount:** Make it attractive to try you out.
3.  **Go where your customers are:** If you offer cleaning, visit homes. If you offer tutoring, go to schools or libraries.
4.  **Post on local social media groups:** Many communities have Facebook groups.
5.  **Word-of-mouth:** Ask happy customers to tell others.
6.  **Offer a small referral bonus:** Give a discount to customers who bring you new business.
7.  **Be visible:** Wear branded clothes (if applicable) or have a clear sign.
8.  **Offer a free consultation or sample:** Let people experience your value.
9.  **Partner with another small business:** Cross-promote each other.
10. **Be persistent and polite:** Don't give up!

## Simple Pricing Example

**Service: Mobile Phone Cleaning & Basic Repair (e.g., screen protector application)**

*   **Phone Cleaning:** $3 - $5
*   **Screen Protector Application:** $2 - $4 (plus cost of protector if you supply it)
*   **Basic Repair (e.g., battery replacement - if you have skills):** $15 - $30 (depending on phone model and part cost)

**Key:** Price based on your time, skill, and what similar services charge.

## Risks and How to Avoid Them

*   **Not enough customers:**
    *   **Avoid:** Talk to more people, improve your marketing, offer better deals.
*   **Running out of money for supplies:**
    *   **Avoid:** Buy only what you need. Ask customers for a small deposit.
*   **Bad reviews or unhappy customers:**
    *   **Avoid:** Listen carefully, do your best work, be honest about what you can do. Apologize and fix mistakes.
*   **Competition:**
    *   **Avoid:** Offer something unique or better service. Focus on your strengths.
*   **Getting paid:**
    *   **Avoid:** Agree on price upfront. Ask for payment upon completion.

## Free Tools to Use

*   **Your phone:** For communication, taking photos, and simple record-keeping.
*   **Social Media:** Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp (for business pages and groups).
*   **Google Docs/Sheets:** For simple record-keeping, lists, and basic planning.
*   **Canva (free version):** For designing simple flyers and social media posts.
*   **Word-of-mouth:** The most powerful free marketing.
*   **Local community notice boards:** For posting flyers.

Not financial advice - do your own research.
