# Free guide: Flutterwave African payments startup valuation and growth

## What it is

Flutterwave is a tech company that helps African businesses accept payments from anywhere in the world. Founded in 2016, it is one of Africa’s biggest startup success stories. Its "valuation and growth" simply refers to how much the company is worth and how fast it expands. 

This guide is not about investing in Flutterwave stock. Instead, it is about using Flutterwave's free tools to grow your own side hustle, freelance gig, or online business as a young African.

## Is it really free (and the catch)?

Yes, creating an account and setting up your dashboard is completely free. 

The catch is that Flutterwave makes money by taking a small percentage fee when you actually make a sale. 
* Local transactions usually cost around 1.4% to 3.8%.
* International card payments cost a bit more (around 3.8% to 4%). 

There are no monthly subscription fees. If you make no sales, you pay nothing.

## How to sign up

1. Go to the official website: **[flutterwave.com](https://flutterwave.com)**.
2. Click on the "Create account" or "Get Started" button.
3. Enter your email address and create a strong password.
4. Select your country and choose whether you are registering as an individual (for freelancers) or a registered business.
5. Verify your email address to unlock your dashboard.

## 5 steps to get value in your first week

1. **Complete your KYC (Know Your Customer):** Upload your ID or government-issued document so your account can receive real money.
2. **Create a Payment Link:** Generate a simple link for a product or service you sell. You do not need a website to do this.
3. **Test the link:** Send the link to a friend or use test mode to see how easy it is for customers to pay you in mobile money or card.
4. **Share on social media:** Paste your payment link in your Instagram, TikTok, or WhatsApp bio so clients can pay you instantly.
5. **Explore the Storefront:** Set up a free mini-online shop inside Flutterwave to display all your products in one neat place.

## Tips

* **Use Flutterwave Store:** If you don't have money to build a fancy website, use their free online store builder.
* **Accept multiple currencies:** Turn on settings to accept USD, GBP, and Euros so you can easily charge global clients.
* **Keep your login safe:** Never share your password or OTP (One-Time Password) with anyone claiming to be customer support.

## Free alternatives

If you want to check out other options for receiving money online, look at:

* **Paystack:** Great for businesses in Nigeria, Ghana, Kenya, and South Africa.
* **Lemonade Finance:** Useful for moving money across African borders.
* **Wise or PayPal:** Good for global freelancing, though withdrawal rules vary by African country.

Not financial advice - do your own research.
