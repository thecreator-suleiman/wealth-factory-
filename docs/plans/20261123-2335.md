# Free business plan: Blockbuster's CEO turned down Netflix deal

# The "Don't Get Blockbustered" Modernization Agency

Blockbuster ignored Netflix and went bankrupt because it failed to adapt to digital change. Millions of traditional businesses face this exact risk today. This business plan helps local, offline shops adapt before modern digital competitors put them out of business.

---

### The Idea in One Line
Help traditional, offline businesses adopt simple digital tools so they stay relevant, find new customers, and avoid going out of business.

---

### Who Pays and Why
* **Who:** Local brick-and-mortar owners (restaurants, grocery stores, salons, auto mechanics, rental businesses).
* **Why:** They see modern online competitors taking their foot traffic. They want more sales and online visibility, but they do not know how to set up tech tools themselves.

---

### Start-up Cost (Low/Realistic, in USD)
You can start this with almost zero capital if you already own a smartphone or computer.

* **Mobile Internet Data:** $15 – $25
* **Transport (visiting local shops):** $10 – $20
* **Printed One-Page Flyers/Portfolios:** $5 – $10
* **Total Estimated Cost:** **$30 – $55**

---

### Step-by-Step: First 30 Days

* **Days 1–7: Master the Basics**
  * Learn how to set up Google Business Profile, WhatsApp Business catalogs, and basic social media pages.
  * Pick one niche to start with (e.g., local dine-in restaurants or hardware shops).
* **Days 8–14: Market Research**
  * Walk around your neighborhood. 
  * List 30 businesses that do not appear on Google Maps or do not have a digital catalog.
* **Days 15–21: Direct Outreach**
  * Visit 5 to 10 shop owners per day in person.
  * Show them on your phone that customers cannot find them online.
  * Pitch a simple digital setup.
* **Days 22–30: Deliver and Review**
  * Complete projects for your first clients.
  * Show them the results (calls received, map views, messages).
  * Collect written reviews or short video testimonials.

---

### How to Get the First 10 Customers

* **The Map Test:** Walk into a shop, search for their service on Google Maps, and show the owner that their competitor appears first.
* **The Free Pilot:** Offer to put them on Google Maps for free. When they see calls coming in, pitch a paid service for full digital setup.
* **The WhatsApp Makeover:** Build a sample WhatsApp product catalog for a vendor in 15 minutes to show immediate value.
* **Word of Mouth:** Give a 20% discount to any client who introduces you to another shop owner on their street.

---

### Simple Pricing Example

Keep your pricing clear and tiered:

* **Starter Setup ($30 – $50 one-time):**
  * Google Maps registration and verification.
  * WhatsApp Business setup (catalog, auto-replies, hours).
* **Growth Package ($75 – $120 one-time):**
  * Starter setup + 5 promotional graphic flyers made on Canva + Facebook/Instagram page optimization.
* **Monthly Retainer ($25 – $40/month):**
  * Update product prices, manage reviews, post weekly promos, and fix account issues.

---

### Risks and How to Avoid Them

* **Risk: Shop owners do not trust technology.**
  * *Fix:* Speak plain language. Do not talk about "SEO" or "digital transformation." Talk about "getting more calls and customer orders."
* **Risk: Clients do not pay on time.**
  * *Fix:* Always collect a 50% deposit before doing any setup work.
* **Risk: Clients expect unrealistic sales overnight.**
  * *Fix:* Make it clear that your job is to make them discoverable. Sales still depend on their product quality and prices.

---

### Free Tools to Use

* **Google Business Profile:** Puts clients on Google Maps for free.
* **WhatsApp Business App:** Turns a phone into an online catalog with automated messaging.
* **Canva (Free Plan):** Design simple menus, banners, and price lists.
* **Google Drive / Sheets:** Track client leads and deliverables.
* **CapCut (Free):** Create simple vertical videos for clients to post on social media.
