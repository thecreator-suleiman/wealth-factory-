# Free business plan: Steve Jobs Apple 1985 Ouster

### The Idea in One Line
Turn the historic 1985 drama of Steve Jobs leaving Apple into viral educational content, digital merchandise, and leadership consulting for young entrepreneurs.

### Who Pays and Why
*   **Students and Young Professionals:** They pay for engaging, bite-sized history lessons and mindset guides because traditional school makes history boring.
*   **Creators and Tech Enthusiasts:** They buy digital wallpapers, posters, and community access because they admire startup culture and tech lore.
*   **Startup Founders:** They pay for workshops on resilience and overcoming business failure, using Steve Jobs’ 1985 exile as a case study.

### Start-Up Cost (Low/Realistic, in USD)
*   **Domain & Hosting:** $15 (via Carrd or WordPress)
*   **Design & Editing Tools:** $0 (Using free tiers of Canva and CapCut)
*   **Marketing (Social Media Ads):** $0 (Organic traffic only)
*   **Total Start-Up Cost:** **$15**

### Step-by-Step: First 30 Days
*   **Days 1–5:** Research the 1985 Apple boardroom coup. Gather iconic quotes, photos, and lesser-known facts.
*   **Days 6–10:** Design 10 short-form video scripts (TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts) breaking down the drama like a movie trailer.
*   **Days 11–15:** Build a simple, one-page landing page offering a free PDF guide: *"3 Leadership Lessons from Steve Jobs’ Biggest Failure."*
*   **Days 16–20:** Film and edit the first 10 videos on a smartphone. Post one video every two days.
*   **Days 21–30:** Engage with comments, refine your messaging based on what goes viral, and launch a $5 digital sticker pack or desktop wallpaper bundle.

### How to Get the First 10 Customers
*   **Leverage Short-Form Video:** Post dramatic storytelling clips about the 1985 ouster on TikTok and Reels with a clear call-to-action in your bio.
*   **Engage in Tech Communities:** Answer questions in Reddit forums (like r/entrepreneur or r/apple) with insightful history, linking back to your free PDF.
*   **Direct Outreach:** Message 20 small-scale tech podcasters or newsletter writers, offering to share the untold story of 1985 for their audience.

### Simple Pricing Example
*   **Product:** "The 1985 Playbook" (A digital PDF + audio summary on how Jobs bounced back to create NeXT and Pixar).
*   **Price:** $9.99 per download.
*   **Goal:** 10 sales = **$99.90 in revenue.**

### Risks and How to Avoid Them
*   **Risk:** Copyright issues with Apple logos and Steve Jobs' image.
*   **Solution:** Use public domain facts, original vector art, and transformative commentary. Do not use official Apple trademarks or copyrighted photos.
*   **Risk:** Low initial views on social media.
*   **Solution:** Focus heavily on emotional storytelling and strong hooks ("How a 30-year-old billionaire got fired from his own company").

### Free Tools to Use
*   **Canva:** For designing social media posts, PDFs, and digital merchandise.
*   **CapCut:** For editing fast-paced, engaging short-form videos with auto-captions.
*   **Carrd:** For building a fast, free, or ultra-cheap landing page.
*   **Mailchimp (Free Tier):** For collecting emails and delivering your free PDF guides.

Not financial advice - do your own research.
