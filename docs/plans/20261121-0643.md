# Free guide: Blockbuster rejected Netflix buyout offer

### What it is

This isn't a modern app where you click buttons to get free cash. Instead, "Blockbuster rejecting Netflix" is a famous business history lesson. 

Back in 2000, Netflix offered to sell itself to Blockbuster for $50 million. Blockbuster laughed and said no. Today, Netflix is worth billions, and Blockbuster is bankrupt. 

Why is this a "free money tool"? It is a free mental model. By studying this historic mistake, young people in Africa and worldwide can learn how to spot massive trends early, avoid arrogant mistakes, and invest in the right modern platforms to build their own wealth. 

### Is it really free (and the catch)?

Yes, learning this history is 100% free. You can read about it on YouTube, Google, or Wikipedia without paying a cent.

The catch? It won’t put money in your pocket today. This is "free money" in the sense of knowledge. If you understand *why* Blockbuster failed, you will avoid putting your hard-earned money into dying companies and trends. 

### How to sign up

You don’t need an account or an email address to use this tool. 

Just open your internet browser and search for "Blockbuster Netflix buyout story." You can watch free documentaries on YouTube or read articles online. 

### 5 steps to get value in your first week

1. **Watch a summary:** Spend 10 minutes on YouTube watching a video about the downfall of Blockbuster. 
2. **Identify the shift:** Notice how Blockbuster relied on physical stores and late fees, while Netflix saw the future of the internet and mail delivery.
3. **Apply it to today:** Ask yourself: What is the "Blockbuster" of today? (Hint: Traditional banks ignoring mobile money or crypto, or old media ignoring TikTok).
4. **Find the "Netflix":** Look for modern, innovative companies solving big problems, especially in fast-growing markets like Africa.
5. **Protect your money:** Make a promise to never invest your savings into old technology just because it is familiar.

### Tips

* **Don't fear small ideas:** When Netflix started, renting DVDs by mail felt weird and slow. Do not dismiss new ideas from young entrepreneurs.
* **Think long-term:** Blockbuster wanted short-term profits from late fees. Netflix focused on long-term subscriber growth. Always think about the next 10 years, not the next 10 days.
* **Keep learning:** History repeats itself in business and finance. Read business case studies weekly.
